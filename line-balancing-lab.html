Theory: line balancing in five formulas
  • CT = available time / demand — the drumbeat of the line (takt).
  • n_min = ⌈Σtᵢ / CT⌉ — you can never do better than this many stations.
  • Efficiency = Σtᵢ / (n · CT) and Balance delay = 1 − Efficiency.
  • Smoothness = √Σ(STmax − STₖ)² — how evenly work is spread (0 = perfect).
  • RPW ranks tasks by positional weight = own time + times of all tasks that follow it, so bottleneck-feeding tasks go early. LCR just grabs the longest eligible task. Most Followers prioritizes tasks that unlock the most successors. None is always best — that's why you test all three.
