Assessment constraints

Complete tasks in order. A hint becomes available only after the first failed attempt. After three incorrect attempts, the correct explanation is shown and the task receives zero points.

Source requirement and teaching assumptions

The Part 08 exercise requires Conveyor 1 to stop when a new part arrives during loading, CNC or unloading. An arrival during CNC must be transferred to Conveyor 2 without CNC processing. Because the slides do not define queue capacity, transfer time or robot-resource details, this model assumes one waiting position, a robot available during CNC, a three-second bypass transfer and a rising-edge pulse named NEW_PART.

Control-training boundary

This activity teaches sequence logic. It is not a safety-system design. Real protective functions require suitable risk assessment and safety-rated equipment.
