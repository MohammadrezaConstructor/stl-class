What question does this challenge answer?

    What you should already know
    • Scan cycle: READ inputs → SOLVE rungs → WRITE outputs, repeated continuously.
    • Contacts are bit tests: NO is TRUE when its bit is 1; NC is TRUE when its bit is 0.
    • Series = AND, parallel branches = OR.
    • On-delay timer: Q becomes 1 when the elapsed time ET reaches the preset PT while IN = 1.
    • Rising edge: TRUE for exactly one scan when a signal changes 0 → 1.

    Source requirement and teaching assumptions

    The Part 08 exercise requires Conveyor 1 to stop when a new part arrives during loading, CNC or unloading. An arrival during CNC must be transferred to Conveyor 2 without CNC processing. Because the slides do not define queue capacity, transfer time or robot-resource details, this model assumes one waiting position, a robot available during CNC, a three-second bypass transfer and a rising-edge pulse named NEW_PART.

    Control-training boundary

    This activity teaches sequence logic. It is not a safety-system design. Real protective functions require suitable risk assessment and safety-rated equipment.

    Glossary of the PLC terms used on this page
    Big picture: from the part at the sensor to the conveyor motor
    1. 1 · Physical deviceA part reaches the photo-eye at the end of Conveyor 1.
    2. 2 · Input moduleIts signal becomes the SN bit in the input image (READ).
    3. 3 · InstructionsEdge detection makes NEW_PART; contacts in R1–R4 and R8 test SN, NEW_PART and the step bits.
    4. 4 · Internal bitsRung results store or update CELL_BUSY, PART_WAITING, BYPASS_ACTIVE and INFEED_HOLD.
    5. 5 · Output imageR5 and R6 write the commands M1, RU and M2 (WRITE).
    6. 6 · ActuatorsThe output module drives the Conveyor 1 contactor, the robot and the Conveyor 2 contactor.

    The stage, waiting and bypass bits are program memory, not physical devices. Only SN comes from the plant, and only M1, RU and M2 go back to it.

    Question by question

      The main question, answered

      A part that arrives while the cell is busy is held at the infeed (INFEED_HOLD turns M1 off), and its one-scan NEW_PART pulse is turned into a stored bit. During loading or unloading the robot is busy, so the part is remembered in PART_WAITING and released by R8 once the main cell is idle. During CNC the robot is free, so BYPASS_ACTIVE sends the part to Conveyor 2 (RU and M2 for 3 s) while STEP_CNC keeps machining the current part. The master stop clears everything. One BOOL can remember only one waiting part.
