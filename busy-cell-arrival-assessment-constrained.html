When another part arrives +

A second part at SN must wait while the cell is busy. The reference program adds the conveyor rules below. If you edit them, the live ladder and values show your applied behavior.

INFEED_HOLD = SN AND CELL_BUSY
M1 = ST AND NOT INFEED_HOLD
M2 = ST AND STEP_UNLOAD
Infeed holdOFF
Bypass request · one scanOFF

A bypass request does not move a part. A physical route, robot availability, stored transfer state and completion signals must be defined first. This lab keeps the second part at the sensor.

A physical part is waiting; this model has no automatic queue. Clear and re-detect it after the cell becomes idle.

Reference model & the three corrections +

Simple: why it now completes

  1. GT = ST AND SN keeps the gate permissive available.
  2. Add TRO.Q to the loading command.
  3. Add TRO.Q to the loading timer enable.

SN is held ON even after pickup, and the gate is allowed to remain commanded. Completing the cycle does not release GT or reset the timers. Remove an enabling condition to rearm.

With the exact eight-rung order, an output is evaluated before its timer. RL/CNS and CNS/RU can therefore overlap for one scan at a transition.

Advanced: remember the operation

NEW_PART = ST AND rising_edge(SN). SN is tracked even with ST off. A held sensor cannot create another arrival when ST is re-enabled.

Stage timers are evaluated before transitions; outputs follow the updated stage. ST off cancels the cycle, clears stage bits and resets timers. It is not pause/resume.

All times are teaching assumptions: 2 / 5 / 10 / 3 seconds. The 50 ms simulated scan is chosen for inspection. Real equipment needs ready, position and completion feedback. This is an educational model.
